# Product and Roadmap

## Product thesis

Start with a trusted typing/conversion utility, convert high-intent candidates into Pro users and expand into recurring institution seats and managed training labs.

## Packaging

- Trial: complete 14-day product evaluation after verified signup.
- Pro: full curriculum, exams, analytics, stenography, advanced conversion and continuity.
- Institution: seats, assignments, lab controls and cohort reporting.

## Release path

1. Production Supabase Auth/JWT and account activation.
2. Trusted Windows Authenticode certificate and signed updater.
3. Clean-machine install/update/restore testing.
4. Controlled candidate cohort and measurable learning validation.
5. Secure billing and entitlement webhook.
6. Institution pilot.

Pricing remains a market hypothesis until billing, refund handling, support capacity and entitlement recovery are implemented.
