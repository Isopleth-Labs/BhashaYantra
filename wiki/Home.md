## Beta status

`0.2.0-beta.1` is an implemented beta candidate. Public distribution stays gated until Windows Authenticode signing, a signed updater, production Supabase configuration, clean-machine testing and legal review are complete.

Built-in lesson and exam passages are original BhashaYantra practice material. Official notices inform rule profiles; the app does not claim its passages are leaked or official past papers.
