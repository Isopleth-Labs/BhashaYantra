# Practice, Tests and Stenography

## Typing curriculum

The beta includes 2,860 original exercises across six ready layouts. Courses progress through Learn Keys, Practice Words, Sentences, Paragraphs and dedicated number/data-entry work.

Practice supports next-key/finger guidance, optional keyboard, non-blocking red error feedback, weak-key tracking, backspace rules, sound and mastery gates.

## Exam workstation

Eighteen configurable profiles cover flexible baselines and official-reference patterns for common recruitment environments. Controls include duration, WPM/KDPH targets, correction rules, highlighting, scrolling, word/keystroke limits, paragraph/tab behavior, custom passages and detailed result cards.

Official notices are linked for rule verification. BhashaYantra passages are original simulations and must not be represented as official or leaked papers.

## Stenography

Hindi and English courtroom/government-office profiles support separate briefing, dictation, transcription and result phases; voice testing; local narration; human audio import; word-aligned scoring; and offline history.

Synthetic narration is labelled. A production catalog of human courtroom audio requires permission/licensing, speaker consent, clear provenance and profile-specific QA before it can be called official or real-court audio.
